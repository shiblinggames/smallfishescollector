Trawls panel: reclaim space — inline slot chip, zone-tinted cards, no tap labels.

Four tightening passes on the Trawls sheet:
- Slot count moves into the header: a compact "N/4" + dot-pip chip sits inline
  with the "Trawls" title and is tappable, opening a new slot-info modal (pips,
  the next-slot Fishing/Nav requirements, and how to raise each). The big
  slot-explainer box is gone from the panel body, freeing a whole block.
- Zone cards drop the "Tap to send crew" / "Tap to collect" labels — the tint
  and glow already carry the affordance — so cards are shorter and cleaner.
  Only running (countdown), locked, and no-free-slot still show a footer line.
- The full-width Ancient Deep card now centers its content (portrait + text +
  any footer) instead of spreading left-to-right.
- Each zone card is tinted with that zone's own accent (shallows blue, open
  green, deep violet, abyss red, ancient violet) instead of the flat neutral;
  ready/running/flashing state accents still override the fill and the border/
  glow signal tappability.
