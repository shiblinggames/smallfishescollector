# Finn: every line on the fishing side

Everything Finn says in the Long Cast, as the Godot game shows it. Generated by `godot/game/tools/finn_script.mjs`; edits go in `content/port_rules.json` (`finn`, `finnAncientBeats`), not here. Mark this file up and the changes are carried over.

Key: **First, the story** is what he tells you before he sets the job (the beat). **Gives it** is what he says setting the job. **Not yet** is what he says while it is open. **Done** is what he says when it is handed back. **Too soon** is what he says when your level is not there yet. A job marked _(port)_ is one the Godot game added. _Italics_ in his lines are his emphasis.

## What you can ask him

Questions that open as he warms to you, one set per standing. You ask, he answers.

### Standing: Another angler

- **You:** Who are you?
  **Finn:** Finn. I fish, I watch, and I am here more than anybody sensible would be.
- **You:** Why watch me?
  **Finn:** Because you are new and I am not, and one of us is going to be interesting to the other.
- **You:** What is worth catching out here?
  **Finn:** Everything, until you have caught it. Then almost nothing. That is the trouble with the shallows.

### Standing: Worth watching

- **You:** How long have you been out here?
  **Finn:** Longer than the harbor has had a name I recognize. I stopped counting on purpose.
- **You:** Do you fish much yourself?
  **Finn:** Every day. Do not ask me what I catch.
- **You:** What is the furthest you have been?
  **Finn:** The lip of the old water. Never over it. Never once over it.

### Standing: Worth testing

- **You:** What is actually down in the Ancient Deep?
  **Finn:** Six things worth the pulling. I have said that before and I will keep saying it until somebody proves me wrong or right.
- **You:** Why does it matter that it is six?
  **Finn:** Because six is a set. Five is a collection and seven is a mess. Six is a thing somebody could finish.
- **You:** What happens if somebody lands all six?
  **Finn:** ...ask me that one again when it is not hypothetical.

### Standing: Worth teaching

- **You:** You said your line comes back empty. Every time?
  **Finn:** Every time. Forty years of every time. You get used to the sound of nothing on the other end.
- **You:** Do you resent me for it?
  **Finn:** Some days. Not most. It turns out watching it happen is closer than never seeing it at all.
- **You:** Why keep setting me jobs?
  **Finn:** Because a hand like yours goes soft if nobody asks anything of it. And because I want to see how far it goes.

### Standing: Nearly his equal

- **You:** Why are you really out here?
  **Finn:** I came looking for somebody who could do the thing I cannot. I have been looking a very long time.
- **You:** And now?
  **Finn:** Now I have found them and I am not entirely sure what I am supposed to feel about it.
- **You:** What do you want from all six?
  **Finn:** To see it. That is what I will tell you today, and it is not the whole of it, and you already knew that.
